About Me

Creative thinking backed by marketing strategy.

My work combines strategic planning, content development, creative execution, and paid advertising. I have worked with businesses in technical training, food exports, education, accounting, mental health, and skincare.

I develop marketing plans around each business's audience, positioning, objectives, and available resources. From building content calendars and writing copy to launching campaigns and reviewing performance, I focus on making every activity serve a clear marketing purpose.

I also use Canva and AI-assisted tools to support visual production while keeping brand consistency and communication goals at the center of the process.
